Introduction Over the past five years, I’ve supported dozens of organizations through cyber crises as an incident response manager. Here’s a conversation I’ve found myself having in almost every ransomware incident: CEO: “Okay… And how long will this take?” Me: “It’s hard to say right now. We only started investigating yesterday.” CEO: “Estimate.” Me: “In cases like this - a full ransomware shutdown - typically I would say between one to two weeks before you can restore operations, safely.
